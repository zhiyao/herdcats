# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in Herdcats, regardless of background,
identity, ability, or experience. Treat people with respect and focus criticism
on the work. Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and
publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope

These standards apply to this project's issues, PRs, reviews, discussions, and
other community spaces, and to people officially representing Herdcats.

## Report an incident

Email [hello@enchantinglabs.com](mailto:hello@enchantinglabs.com) privately with
the relevant links, what happened, and any immediate safety concerns. Avoid
posting incident details or personal information publicly. Maintainers will
review reports fairly and limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate
and respond. If a maintainer is involved, say so in the report.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content and apply a correction, written warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition.
Serious incidents may warrant immediate removal. Respect moderation decisions
and restrictions; appeals can be sent to the same private contact address.
